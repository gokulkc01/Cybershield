## ✨ WHAT MAKES THIS UNIQUE

### NOT a simple classifier
CyberShield is a **behavioral adversarial robustness research platform** that:

1. **Investigates** behavioral properties under adversarial pressure
2. **Demonstrates** robustness through interactive visualization
3. **Quantifies** resilience with invariance scores
4. **Visualizes** feature shifts and behavioral changes
5. **Communicates** scientific findings to non-technical stakeholders

### The Mutation Lab ⭐
Is the **primary differentiator** showing:
- How mutations affect detection
- Behavioral resilience under pressure
- Feature-level analysis of changes
- Quantified robustness scoring

This **visual storytelling** is what makes CyberShield special.
